== League of Legends V26.21 ==
=== Client ===
;Minimum System Requirements
* We've shipped an improvement to how our patch launcher works. This improvement uses a software library that requires SSE 4.2, which results in us increasing our AMD minspec by one version.

;Mythic Shop
* As a heads up, during Patch 26.22, we'll be making changes to the Mythic Shop so that each player will start to see offerings unique to them, rather than everyone having the same selection at a given time. We're hoping this will open up the possibility in the future to allow for you to see more relevant content. 
* In preparation for this, we'll be making some changes during 26.21 so the rotation timings of the Bi-weekly, Weekly, and Daily sections of the Mythic Shop are better aligned. Keep a close eye on the rotation timings so you don't miss anything.

;Replays
* Replay downloads will be limited to 10 per hour. You will still be able to download your own replays, as well as replays from your friends and other players.
* This change is necessary to prevent large-scale abuse of downloaded replays by bad actors using vibecoded apps to scrape thousands upon thousands of replays. We opted for a time-based limit to still allow creators to make content, while preventing mass exploitative downloads. We'll continue to monitor how many replays are downloaded to ensure players have access to this important feature while preventing clear misuse.
